Approval queue

The agent can propose trades but cannot execute one on its own. Clicking Accept mints a single-use confirmation key and hands it to the agent, which is the only way an order reaches Alpaca. Your holdings and reports are not changed by a trade — submit a new report afterwards to pick up the new position.
