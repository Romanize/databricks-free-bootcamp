Weather Intelligence

National Weather Service alerts and forecasts, embedded into Lakebase (Postgres + pgvector) and retrieved by cosine similarity.

1. Harvest & embed

Sync writes raw text into weather_documents. Embedding chunks it, runs all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and writes 384-dim vectors into weather_embeddings (first run downloads the model, so it can take a minute).
